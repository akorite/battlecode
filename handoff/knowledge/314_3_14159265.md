## Strategy theory — the wall-pusher

3.14159265 is the weirdest elite fingerprint: **61% of its dragons die crashing into kelp walls** — and it wins 65% anyway. Zero suicides, 3.77 pings/action (near-saturation sonar), highest split count among the no-suicide camp (311/g), and the *lowest* pearl intake of the top ten (169/game — it doesn't farm, it fights for space).

Two readings, probably both true: (1) its dragons hug kelp edges deliberately — walls are safe from one direction of attack, and pearl-dense pockets sit behind kelp in this mapgen; pushing the edge is how it contests territory other bots avoid; (2) it sprints hard through contested corridors — a dragon at speed can't stop for a wall that appears mid-move, and 61% wall-deaths is what aggressive boundary play costs.

The eval curve tells a comeback story: it *dips* mid-game (0.59 → 0.58 flat through the middle) then surges — it cedes the clean mid-game position and wins the knife fight at the boundary late. Its lost_len gap (+15.1) is the highest of the non-suicide teams — it spends dragons on map control. Schooltime is its nightmare (44%): a small open map with no walls to exploit.
