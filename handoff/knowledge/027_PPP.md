## Strategy theory — the boom-bust overextender

PPP's alive curve is the dataset's most dramatic: it peaks at ~30 dragons right at mid-game, then **collapses to ~19 by 65% and stays flat** — a visible over-extension crash where the swarm outgrows its support and dies in a wave (341 deaths/game — the most of any top team). It spends the crash productively though: 64 suicides/game says part of that collapse is deliberate harvesting.

The eval curve matches the boom-bust: it sags at mid-game (0.57 at 50%) — eval sees the over-extension — then recovers as the consolidated remainder plays out the win. PPP's identity is *early aggression*: it splits most per game among non-Cutlery teams (353/g) and its kill style is h2h-heavy early. The collapse is the cost of the opening.

Fatal flaw is map-shaped: Devil at 27% win rate — the smallest/tightest map set punishes over-extension hardest. Its Trauma rate is 90% — open chaos lets the boom pay off.
