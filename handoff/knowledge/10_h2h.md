## Rock-paper-scissors reads

**Cutlery is kingmaker-by-mass:** beats everyone except Sponge (16–18 — the only elite that out-sits it) and roughly breaks even with Cache. Its web is hostile to coordinated styles — forgot 43–37, calc 38–27, Stockfish 28–17.

**3.14159265 has the strangest bracket:** loses to the top two (19–29 Cutlery, 18–25 forgot) but *dominates* the defensive teams — 30–10 vs Sponge (the season's most lopsided elite record) and 19–14 vs SSS. Wall-edge play flanks compact defenses; mass webs suffocate the wall-pusher.

**forgot vs Cache (53–43) is the doctrine showdown:** collection beats biomass when volumes are comparable — the suicide engine's pearls matter more than Cache's churn.

**cheji is the gatekeeper of the elite tier:** positive or even against most (34–31 forgot, 13–7 SSS, 17–9 Cache, 11–9 Stockfish) but loses to Cutlery (21–29) — its lockdown needs borders Cutlery's web doesn't give it.

**SSS vs forgot (49–51 over 100 games)** is the season's closest elite rivalry — the precision-vs-economy debate, statistically dead even.

**PPP is a counter-fighter:** 19–9 vs 3.14 (wall-pushers fold to over-extension since they can't fall back), but bleeds to Cutlery and forgot.

**Cache feeds on chaos:** it owns Sponge 19–10 and lost to calc 1–10 — biomass beats defense, loses to organized mass.

**calc's only real check:** Cutlery (27–38). Everyone else it bulldozes — 76.5% WR isn't inflated.

## What the matrix says about the meta

There's no dominant strategy — the top is a genuine wheel: mass beats coordination, coordination beats defense, defense beats chaos, chaos beats precision, and the corpse economy sits orthogonal to all of it. Matchups like PPP-vs-3.14 (19–9) or 3.14-vs-Sponge (30–10) are larger than elo differences would predict — **style matters as much as strength** at the top of the ladder.
