## Strategy theory — the efficiency minimalist

SSS is the anti-swarm: the fewest splits in the top ten (170/g, barely half of Cutlery), the fewest deaths (153/g), sonar at a modest 3.15→2.2 — and it still holds 2110 elo. Its feat snapshot at mid-game is remarkable: **negative economy differentials** — out-eaten (−8.3), out-deathed is *lower* (−5.2 fewer deaths, i.e. it loses fewer dragons), lost_len −9.8 (it loses less length) — SSS wins while conceding the resource war.

Read: SSS plays *precision over mass*. Its dragons survive (it has the cleanest hitWall profile of the suicide-less teams at 0%), trades heads deliberately (h2h 26% — highest among elites relative to its low death count), and loses to box-ins rarely (14%). The eval curve is flat at ~0.50-0.55 through mid-game then climbs to 0.61 — close games decided late, on execution not position. Its elim rate is 52% — it kills cleanly when it kills.

The 60.6% win rate — lowest in the top ten — shows the ceiling of the style: elegant minimalism beats weaker bots reliably but can't out-produce the swarm engines in a bad matchup. Dead-even with forgot to mention (49–51) over 100 games — the most balanced top-tier rivalry in the dataset.
