# The doctrines — strategy families ranked by evidence

*A doctrine is a repeatable strategic identity visible in the counters. These are the eight the season produced.*

## 1. Mass swarm — split maximalism

**Practitioners:** Cutlery (464 splits/g), calc (264→48.2 peak), Cache (334), bread first search (316).

The base technology of the season: split relentlessly, claim tiles with bodies, win the bodies logit. Every elite has it; what separates them is what they do *on top of it*. Pure mass alone caps around ~70% WR — it's necessary, not sufficient.

## 2. Corpse economy — suicide → feed

**Practitioners:** forgot (206/g), cheji (123/g), PPP (64/g), WeHaveQuizzes (39/g), ddabap (35/g).

The elite differentiator. Converts dead dragons into ⌈L/2⌉ pearls on the corpse tiles, then a champion harvests them — length concentrated, exactly what the bell scores. The mechanism that lets a swarm keep "living" after it's dead.

**Only works with collection.** Mid-tier teams suicide without the champion loop and just lose dragons — it's a whole-strategy commitment, not a tech add-on.

## 3. Sonar saturation — the sensor platform

**Practitioners:** calc (3.92), Sabotage-d (3.91), Stockfish (3.83), 3.14159265 (3.78), PPP (3.86), ddabap (3.87), DESTINY 3 (3.81), w daniel ma (3.91).

Information-maximalism: spend ~97% of the channel every action. These teams tend toward crash-prone death profiles (wall/self ≥60% combined) — vision funds aggression, aggression costs hulls. Not strictly necessary (Cutlery runs 2.35), but present in most top-10 builds.

## 4. No-sonar biomass — deaf mass

**Practitioners:** Cache (0.40), Sponge (1.27), Team SKKU (1.02), tungtung67 (0.61), 中国必须人能飞 (1.05), Computers (1.09).

The counter-intuitive family: win without talking. Cache is the proof of concept — 2038 elo, 69% WR, 7% channel usage. Its swarm persists (flat ~31 alive curve) because mass itself is coordination — dragons don't need to know where to go if there's always a friendly body in the right place. Hard ceiling though: no champion hunting, no reactions — they grind, not adapt.

## 5. Wall-edge play — living on kelp

**Practitioners:** 3.14159265 (61% hitWall), calc (58%), zitian (51%), Sabotage-d (~50%).

Territory along kelp is attackable from ≤3 sides — these teams deliberately operate at the boundary and accept wall-deaths as overhead. A real, validated choice: 3.14 is top-3 on this diet. The failure mode is not the wall itself but maps without exploitable walls (3.14 at 44% on Schooltime).

## 6. Efficiency minimalism — precision over mass

**Practitioners:** SSS (170 splits/g, 153 deaths/g, both lowest in top 10), y (202/g, 212 splits/g).

Concedes the resource war (SSS is out-eaten, out-massed at mid-game) and wins on execution: low death count, clean trades, wins decided at the margin. Highest purity, lowest ceiling — 60% WR because it can't out-produce the swarm engines when the board gets big.

## 7. Defensive bell play — the spoiler

**Practitioner:** Sponge (the only one).

Flat eval curve until the 80% mark, never blows up, wins on the 500-round length tiebreak. Negative mid-game feature diffs, positive results. 59% WR — capped but annoyingly consistent; the worst matchup for wall-pushers would be it if it weren't for 3.14's 30-10 h2h edge.

## 8. Sprint — dead technology

**Practitioners:** nobody (0.2% global share). Only cheji bt uses it meaningfully (1.2%).

Verdict: retired. Body is currency; speed is a luxury only a feeding loop can amortize. Sprint survives as a micro-tech inside the corpse economy — not as a doctrine.

## Doctrine interactions (h2h evidence)

- **Corpse economy > chaos biomass:** forgot 53–43 vs Cache — collection beats mass when volumes equal.
- **Wall-edge > defensive bell:** 3.14 30–10 vs Sponge — the most lopsided elite matchup; edge play flanks compact defense.
- **Mass swarm ≈ corpse economy:** Cutlery 43–37 vs forgot — roughly even; raw splitting holds its own.
- **Efficiency ≈ corpse economy:** SSS 49–51 vs forgot over 100 games — precision survives the suicide engine.
- **Mass > sensor platform:** Cutlery 28–17 vs Stockfish; calc 7–3 vs Stockfish — vision loses to volume at the top.
