## Ranks 11–20 quick reads

- **WeHaveQuizzes** — runs a mini-forgot (39 sui/g) with a much lower harvest (147 eaten — lowest in top 20). Suicide without collection = the doctrine's failure mode.
- **Team SKKU** — biggest top-20 casualty of the no-sonar approach: 265 splits, 246 deaths, 54% WR. The mass works, the silence costs.
- **zitian** — the wall-rider archetype at rank 13: 51% hitWall, 68% WR, 156 pearls eaten (it fights for walls, doesn't farm them).
- **中国必须人能飞** — profile of pure quiet splitting: 252 splits, ~1.05 sonar, nothing else notable. Flat doctrine; the baseline for "what splits alone buys" (54.8%).
- **bread first search** — the churn monster: 316 splits + 303 deaths + 3.04 sonar. High-volume brawler that trades bodies for tempo.
- **DESTINY 3** — sonar-saturated conservative: 3.81 pings but only 217 splits/206 deaths and 42.2 peak — information hoarded, aggression banked.
- **ddabap** — self-rammer variant: 296 deaths (many self), 3.87 sonar, 35 sui — the biomass family with sensors.
- **Sabotage-d** — the most balanced elite profile: 3.91 sonar + 274 splits + 225 eaten, ~50% wall deaths, 68.9% WR. Best WR-per-risk in the 1900s.
- **y** — the gatekeeper: kills the weak (234–62 vs zitian) and loses to the strong — the ladder's sorting hat at rank 19.
- **tungtung67** — the silent farmer: 0.61 sonar but 249 pearls eaten (highest harvest in 11–20); passive economy, no fights.

## The notable-vs-elite line

Between ranks 5 and 11 sits the season's real divide: ranks 1–10 all average ≥42-peak swarms *or* a deliberate doctrine (SSS's minimalism); ranks 11–20 are competent versions of the same ideas at ~70-80% the volume. There is no hidden tech below rank 10 — the gap is execution.
